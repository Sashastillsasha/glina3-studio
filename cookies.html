Cookies & Local Storage

Checked against repository code on 8 November 2026. Review this page whenever analytics, embeds, fonts, or other scripts change.

Game score

The mini-game stores your best score for the current day in your browser’s local storage under a GLINA³-specific key. It is used only to show your daily best on this device. It is not sent to GLINA³ and remains until you or your browser clear site data. The game does not require this storage to use the rest of the website. You can clear it using your browser’s site-data controls.

Website analytics

The site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics using a JavaScript beacon. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage for usage metrics. The beacon is still a third-party request, so technical request information may be processed to deliver the service. See Cloudflare’s privacy notice and our Privacy Policy.

Fonts and external services

The current site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a browser requests these files, Google receives technical connection data such as the IP address. The site also links to external websites; those services apply their own privacy and cookie practices when you visit them.

Cookie choices

The code reviewed for this page does not load advertising pixels, embedded social-media feeds, or other tracking-cookie tools. If GLINA³ adds a service that requires consent, it should be blocked until visitors can make a free and informed choice, with an equally easy “reject” option.
