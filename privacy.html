Who is responsible for your data

GLINA3, a Dutch general partnership (vennootschap onder firma / VOF), is responsible for personal data processed through this website. KvK number 98443933. Contact: office@glina3.nl. See Legal / Company Details for the verified business address and VAT ID.

Data we receive

This website does not submit your email address to a mailing-list service. If you use the updates form, your browser opens an email draft addressed to GLINA³. Your email app sends nothing until you choose to send it. If you send the message, GLINA³ receives your email address and any content you include.

The page is published through GitHub Pages, based on the repository’s public website configuration. The Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon also loads from Cloudflare. These providers may process technical request information needed to serve the page and report usage. The exact processing roles, retention periods and transfer safeguards depend on the active GitHub and Cloudflare account settings and terms, which GLINA³ still needs to verify.

Why we use data

  • To reply to enquiries or send studio updates when you have asked to receive them
  • To operate, secure and understand use of the website
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply

For marketing updates, the basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing office@glina3.nl with “Unsubscribe” in the subject, or by replying to an update. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing.

Who may receive data

Website requests go to GitHub Pages; the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon is served by Cloudflare. If you send the form email, it is handled by the email service configured on your device and by GLINA³’s office@glina3.nl mailbox, which uses Google Workspace. Verify the current data-processing terms, subprocessors, international transfer safeguards and account retention settings with GitHub, Cloudflare and Google before treating this notice as final.

How long we keep it

The website itself does not retain a mailing-list or enquiry submission: the form opens a draft in the visitor’s email app, and no message is sent until they choose to send it. Sent messages are received in GLINA3’s Google Workspace mailbox. The Google Workspace Admin Console currently shows that automatic deletion of email and chat messages is switched off for GLINA3. Messages that a user moves to Trash are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days. This describes the service settings; it does not set GLINA3’s own retention policy for active mailbox messages. GLINA3 should delete enquiry emails and update-subscription records when they are no longer needed for their original purpose, and document a concrete internal schedule. If you subscribed to updates, you can ask us to unsubscribe or delete your contact details at any time by emailing office@glina3.nl. Invoices and records required for accounting are kept separately for the statutory Dutch tax-retention period, generally 7 years; they are not used as a mailing list.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Contact office@glina3.nl. You may also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the website or our data practices change. For privacy questions, contact office@glina3.nl.
